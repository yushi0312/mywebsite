Our story

Kopi & Kin started in 2024 as a tiny takeaway counter run by two siblings, Aiman and Mei Ling. "Kopi" is for our parents' traditional coffee stall, and "Kin" is for family - because that is how we treat our regulars.

Today we roast twice a week and serve everything from a classic kopi-o to single-origin pour-overs. Stay for five minutes or five hours; the corner seat by the window is yours.
